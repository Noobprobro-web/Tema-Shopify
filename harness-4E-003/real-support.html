Before you write

How do I switch between the Sartorial, Edition and Archive presets?

Open your theme in the editor, go to theme settings and choose a preset at the top of the panel. Presets change settings only, so your section content stays where it is.

How do 3D models and augmented reality work?

Upload your .glb or .gltf files to the product media gallery in the Shopify admin. The theme starts the Shopify XR viewer on supported devices without any extra setup.

How do I set up colour swatches for variants?

The theme reads native Shopify category swatches. Categorise the option as colour in the admin and define the colour or swatch image on the variant.
