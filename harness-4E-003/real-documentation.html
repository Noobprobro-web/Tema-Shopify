Quick start and installation

Upload the theme and open the editor.

The theme is built for Online Store 2.0 with native vanilla JavaScript, so there is no framework to install and nothing to configure before the first preview.

  1. Download the theme zip file from your Shopify account or your Theme Store purchase.
  2. In the Shopify admin, go to Online store > Themes.
  3. Under Theme library, choose Add theme > Upload zip file.
  4. Select the zip file and upload it.
  5. Choose Customize to open the theme editor.

Theme presets

Three starting points for different houses.

Each preset changes theme settings only, so switching does not move or delete the content of your sections.

Default

Sartorial

Warm editorial elegance for tailoring and bespoke fashion. Alabaster grounds, rich contrast and a serif voice.

Preset

Edition

Crisp architectural minimalism. High contrast type, sharp rules and gallery whitespace for contemporary fashion and accessories.

Preset

Archive

Heritage warmth with deeper tones and dramatic composition, for vintage curation, leather goods, watches and legacy houses.

Tip

To change preset, open Theme settings and pick one from the preset list at the top of the panel.

Colour schemes and typography

Where the palette and the type scale live.

Colour schemes are configured in Theme settings > Colours, and every section can carry its own scheme. Body and heading fonts, scale and weight live in Theme settings > Typography, with system fallbacks handled for you.

The shipped schemes hold the contrast ratios that accessibility review asks for: 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for interface boundaries. If you change the colours, keep that in mind.

Signature sections

The pieces that carry the storefront.

  • Hero editorial: full viewport and split banners with separate desktop and mobile images, focal point control and alignment settings.
  • Complete the look: shoppable bundles that add several products to the cart at once, each with its own variant picker.
  • Lookbook: hotspots over a photograph that open product cards with live price and availability.
  • Curated showcase: tabbed collections with keyboard support.
  • Featured product: a standalone product with variant pickers, gallery and accelerated checkout.

Product page and media

Models, swatches, quantity and pickup.

  • 3D models and augmented reality: upload .glb or .gltf files to the product media gallery and the viewer starts on supported devices.
  • Colour swatches: native Shopify category swatches, with either a colour or a swatch image.
  • Line total: the total for the chosen quantity is recalculated as the shopper changes it, without touching the unit price above.
  • Gift card recipient: name, email, message and send date for digital gift cards.
  • Pickup availability: live local stock and pickup times.

Cart drawer

What the slide-out cart can do.

  • Free shipping progress with a threshold you set.
  • Order notes, terms checkbox and discount display.
  • Accelerated checkout buttons.
  • Quantity steppers that keep the cart in step with the server even under fast repeated taps.

Predictive search and filters

How shoppers find things.

Search suggests products, collections, pages and articles as the shopper types, through Shopify’s own predictive search.

Collection and search pages carry faceted filters that read prices written with a comma decimal separator and announce every result change to screen readers.
