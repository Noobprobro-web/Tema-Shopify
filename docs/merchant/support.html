Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between Sartorial, Edition, and Archive presets?

Go to your Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes > Customize. Open the Theme Settings panel (gear icon on the left sidebar) and select your desired preset from the preset selector at the top.

How do 3D models and AR work in Sartorial?

Simply upload your .glb or .gltf 3D model files directly to your Product Media gallery in the Shopify Admin. Sartorial automatically initializes the Shopify XR viewer on mobile devices.

How do I configure color swatches for product variants?

Sartorial natively reads Shopify Category Swatches. Ensure your product options are categorized as "Color" in the Shopify Admin, and define your colors or swatch images directly in the Shopify Variant settings.
