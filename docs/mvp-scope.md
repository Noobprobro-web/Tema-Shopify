# MVP Scope

## Target Merchant

Premium product brands with strong visual identity and catalogues from small to medium size.

Primary categories:

- Fashion and accessories.
- Jewelry.
- Skincare and wellness.
- Home decor.
- Coffee, wine and specialty goods.

## Required Templates

- Home.
- Product.
- Collection.
- Search.
- Cart.
- Page.
- Blog.
- Article.
- 404.
- Gift card if required by final Shopify checks.

## Core Sections

- Header.
- Footer.
- Announcement bar.
- Hero editorial.
- Featured collection.
- Product grid.
- Featured product.
- Image with text.
- Editorial story.
- Collection list.
- Product recommendations.
- Testimonials or press strip.
- Newsletter.
- Rich text.
- FAQ.

## Product Page MVP

- Media gallery.
- Title, price, vendor optional.
- Variant picker.
- Quantity selector.
- Add to cart.
- Buy buttons if supported.
- Pickup/availability placeholder if applicable.
- Product description.
- Collapsible details.
- Complementary or related products.

## Collection Page MVP

- Collection hero.
- Sort.
- Filtering plan compatible with Shopify.
- Responsive product grid.
- Product cards with image swap, swatches where available and quick add where safe.

## Presets MVP

- Atelier: premium editorial neutral.
- Wellness: soft, clean, sensory.
- Studio: sharp, monochrome, product-first.

## Deferred

- Complex animations.
- Advanced predictive search.
- Deep product comparison.
- Loyalty/subscription app integrations.
- Heavy 3D/WebGL effects.
